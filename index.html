Glossary of the Table

press G to show or hide during play
Life Total. Drop to 0 and you lose. Damage and payments reduce it; lifelink and restorers raise it.
Mana. The resource that pays for spells, drawn mostly from tapping lands. Five colors: white, blue, black, red, green.
The Stack. Where spells and abilities wait after being cast. Resolves last-in, first-out; anyone may respond.
Priority. The right to act. It passes between players before anything resolves.
Tap. Turn a permanent sideways to show its cost is paid. Untaps at your untap step.
Summoning Sickness. A creature can’t attack or tap the turn it arrives. Haste ignores it.
Upkeep. Step after untap, before draw. “Beginning of your upkeep” triggers fire here.
Draw Step. You draw one card each turn. Drawing from an empty library loses the game.
Main Phase. Where lands are played and sorceries cast. Two per turn: before and after combat.
Combat Phase. Beginning of combat, declare attackers, declare blockers, damage, end of combat.
First Strike. Deals combat damage before creatures without it.
Double Strike. First strike and normal damage. Two hits.
Deathtouch. Any amount of damage from this source destroys a creature.
Trample. Excess combat damage spills past blockers to the player or planeswalker.
Lifelink. Whenever this source deals damage, you gain that much life.
Flying. Only creatures with flying or reach can block it.
Reach. Can block creatures with flying.
Haste. Can attack and tap the turn it enters the battlefield.
Vigilance. Attacking doesn’t tap this creature.
Menace. Can’t be blocked except by two or more creatures.
Hexproof. Opponents’ spells and abilities can’t target it. You still can.
Ward N. When an opponent targets it, they pay N or the spell is countered.
Defender. Can’t attack.
Flash. Cast any time you could cast an instant, even on an opponent’s turn.
Counter a Spell. Remove it from the stack before it resolves. It goes to the graveyard and does nothing.
Scry N. Look at the top N cards of your library; put any of them back or on the bottom.
Surveil N. Look at the top N cards; put them into your graveyard or back on top.
Mill. Put cards from the top of a library directly into the graveyard.
Exile. Removed from the game entirely. Not the graveyard; far harder to bring back.
Planeswalker. An ally with loyalty counters. Its abilities add or remove loyalty; at zero it leaves.
Commander Damage. In Commander, 21 combat damage from the same commander kills a player, whatever their life total.
Poison Counters. Ten and you lose. Infect and toxic hand them out; lifegain doesn’t help.
Mulligan. Shuffle back and draw a new opening hand, one card fewer each time.
Legend Rule. Two legendary permanents with the same name: keep one, the rest go.
Land Drop. You may play one land per turn.
Ways to Win. Life to 0, ten poison counters, 21 commander damage, drawing from an empty library, or concession.
